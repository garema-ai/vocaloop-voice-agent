HOW THIS MVP WORKS

VocaLoop uses the browser's speech recognition and text-to-speech features, with a small curated vocabulary bank and transparent answer rules. No account, uploads, or AI API keys required.

Prototype for an Associate Product Manager application. Learning interactions are client-side. Speech recognition availability and processing depend on your browser; avoid speaking private information.
